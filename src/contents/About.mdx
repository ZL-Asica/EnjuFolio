I am **Elara Liu**, a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Informatics at **Emory University**, working with [Azra Ismail](https://www.azraismail.me/). I previously earned an M.S. in Computer Science from **Northwestern University**, advised by [Haoqi Zhang](https://www.haoqizhang.com/) and [Karan Ahuja](https://karan-ahuja.com/), and a B.S. in Computer Science and Informatics from **UC Irvine**.

My research lies at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)**, **Health Informatics**, and **Computer-Supported Cooperative Work (CSCW)**. I study how interactive and intelligent systems can support people in everyday health contexts, particularly when access to information, resources, and care is uneven. My current work explores **human-centered AI**, patient-clinician communication and collaboration, and the design of accessible health technologies for **underserved and historically underrepresented populations**, with the broader goal of advancing **health equity**.
